1.3 Project metadata

1.3.1 Species

  • Barbary macaque (Macaca sylvanus)
  • Ugandan red colobus (Piliocolobus tephrosceles)
  • common noctule bat (Nyctalus noctula)
  • Greater noctule bat (Nyctalus lasiopterus)

1.3.2 Location

Samples are collected at Mpala Research Station, Laikipia, Kenya, within the baboon trapping and collaring efforts of the MBRP.

1.3.3 Dates

Samples were collected:

  • 2024
  • 2025
  • 2026
